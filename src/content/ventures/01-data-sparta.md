Most small businesses have their numbers spread across spreadsheets, accounting software and a few people's heads. Data Sparta pulls it into one place they can trust, then helps them use AI on it without wasting money.

I started it after seeing small organisations priced out of decent data work: fair prices, work shaped around the business, and a phone that gets answered. Clients deal with the person who does the work.

I own each engagement end to end, from discovery with stakeholders through to delivery and support. Clients so far include:

- **A leading Australian horse auctioneer:** a unified data platform, a recommendation engine and automated sale reporting.
- **State sporting organisations:** third-party system integrations, Medallion-architecture platforms, self-service analytics and secure AI agent access.
