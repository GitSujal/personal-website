A training academy that closes the gap between academic knowledge and the job. It doesn't stop at teaching tools. Over twelve weeks students build a real project portfolio and learn Python, SQL and Power BI, alongside problem solving, communication and data storytelling.

After that comes career support: resume reviews, interview preparation and referrals.
