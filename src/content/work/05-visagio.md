At Visagio I worked out where $40M a year of absenteeism at BHP was hiding. I combined time-and-attendance analysis with site visits to see how people actually got onto site, then built a tool that matched access patterns against rosters and leave records. Supervisors could reconcile each flagged absence as leave.
