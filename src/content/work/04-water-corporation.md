The model predicts the moisture content coming out of treatment-plant centrifuges from real-time SCADA data: rotational speed, intake flow, temperature and more. It won a top paper at OzWater 2024. I took it from prototype to live operations with an automated MLOps pipeline under strict cross-account governance, so a new version reaches production in minutes instead of days.
